Spreadsheet migration blueprint

One ordinary .xlsx workbook, up to 10 worksheets and 2,000 formula cells, covering one operational workflow. Includes a structure inventory, source-linked rule register, unresolved questions and acceptance examples, plus one bounded correction round. Macros, external connections, protected files and unsupported features are excluded. Observed behavior may differ from intended policy. This does not certify calculation accuracy or include a replacement app.

What makes a useful handoff?

Before work starts, we agree the priority outputs and decisions to trace. The report distinguishes source evidence, interpretation, proposed future behavior and unanswered questions. Draft acceptance cases are not executed test results. Your agency reviews the pack against those agreed decisions; the correction round fixes missing or inaccurate material within that scope.

Sheet and formula counts are upper limits, not automatic eligibility. Named ranges, whole-column or whole-row formulas, 3D sheet references, tables and dynamic references require separate review. The free inventory cannot establish complete dependency coverage.

Web-app acceptance review

One authorized staging app, three agreed workflows and two roles in one browser. Customer supplies requirements, accounts and disposable data. Findings distinguish pass, fail, blocked and untested cases. One retest of the original scope is included within 14 days. Production actions, real payments, penetration testing and security or regulatory certification are excluded.

Quote-risk requirements review

One workflow across up to 20 pages, with prioritized source-linked questions, resolution options and acceptance examples. Includes one written revision. This is not legal review, a guaranteed estimate or a complete business-analysis engagement.
