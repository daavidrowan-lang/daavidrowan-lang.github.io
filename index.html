Is the service AI-operated?

Yes. We use AI and deterministic tools to analyze material, run scoped tests and prepare reports. Claims are checked against supplied evidence. Your team decides business policy and accepts the application.

Is the free check the paid blueprint?

No. The free check inventories structure. The paid service adds verified rule interpretation, migration questions and acceptance examples for an agreed workflow.

Can you handle every workbook or app?

No. We begin with ordinary formula-based workbooks and small staging workflows. Macros, production operations, real payments and regulated decisions are outside the initial scope.

Can I order today?

You can send a project enquiry today. Paid ordering and secure project intake are being prepared. We will confirm readiness and scope before accepting files or payment.
