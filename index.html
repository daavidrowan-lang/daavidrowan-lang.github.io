A person behind the work

Talk to David.

David leads Rowan Workshop, using AI extensively for analysis, testing and drafting alongside deterministic tools and source checks. David can step in when needed; human involvement is agreed for each project.

Looking for a quick inventory first? Use the free local workbook check. It makes no upload and does not replace the paid pack.

Is this right for every workbook?

No. Macros, external connections, protected files and unsupported or high-stakes material are outside the initial service. A sheet/formula count alone does not establish eligibility. See the limits.

What does the client still decide?

Whether the recorded behavior is the intended future policy. We document the evidence and open questions; your team approves the specification and accepts the replacement.

Can I place an order today?

You can send a project enquiry. Paid ordering and confidential intake are being prepared. Readiness, scope and arrangements are confirmed before accepting source files or payment.

How does this compare with our own analysis?

Compare the complete sample with what your team already produces. The paid pack is intended to supply a finished handoff, rather than software for you to operate. If the free inventory or your own process is sufficient, use that.
