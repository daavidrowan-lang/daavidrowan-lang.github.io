Privacy, in plain language.

The local workbook check

Your selected workbook is processed in your browser. This tool does not transmit the workbook or its analysis to Rowan Workshop or an AI provider. It does not save the file in browser storage. Clear removes the current selection and report. Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them.

Website visits

This site adds no advertising pixels or third-party analytics. Hosting and network providers process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and access logs, under their own policies. The public domain uses GitHub Pages. Ordinary web visits are not necessarily anonymous.

Project enquiries

The enquiry form prepares an email in your email app or copies text to your clipboard. It does not submit that text to a server. Email you send to daavidrowan@gmail.com is received and processed through Gmail. Send a project description first. Do not include passwords, customer datasets or confidential attachments.

If you arrive through a recognized campaign link, the page displays its campaign label and includes that label in the email draft or copied enquiry. It is not sent automatically. Arbitrary URL parameters and advertising click IDs are not copied into your enquiry. No campaign cookie or local storage is used.

AI assistance and human involvement

David uses AI to help with enquiries, analysis, testing and drafting, and can step in directly when needed. Before confidential project material is processed, we confirm the tools involved, the processing arrangements and any agreed human involvement.

Paid project materials

Secure intake, processing terms and payment arrangements are being prepared. Before a paid review, you will receive the scope, required inputs, processing arrangements and retention terms. Do not send source files or credentials before these are confirmed. Customer material will not be reused publicly without permission.

Questions or deletion requests

Email daavidrowan@gmail.com about privacy or a previous enquiry. Identify the relevant message if requesting deletion. Necessary business records may require separate retention.
